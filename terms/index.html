What TipOut is

TipOut helps servers and bartenders keep a personal record of hours and tips and see estimates such as take-home pay, average hourly earnings and bar tip splits. TipOut is a record-keeping tool. It is not tax, legal, accounting or financial advice, and it doesn’t replace your employer’s payroll records. Check anything important, such as the amounts you report for taxes, against your official records.

Your data

What you log belongs to you. We use it only to run TipOut for you, as described in our Privacy Policy.

Receipt scanning

Scan receipt uses a third-party AI service (Anthropic) to read the numbers on photos you take, and only after you agree in the app. AI can misread numbers, so always review what it fills in before saving. Only scan checkouts that you’re allowed to photograph, and don’t use scanning to collect other people’s information. Scanning has a daily limit and may change or pause as we improve it.

TipOut Pro and scan packs

  • TipOut Pro is an auto-renewing subscription, monthly or yearly, bought through Apple. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm. It renews automatically unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and your account is charged for renewal within the 24 hours before it ends. Manage or cancel it anytime in your iPhone’s Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • A free trial, when offered, turns into a paid subscription at its end unless you cancel before then. Any unused part of a trial ends when you buy a subscription.
  • Scan packs are one-time purchases of receipt scans. They don’t expire, can’t be transferred, and are used after your monthly scans.
  • Free accounts get 10 receipt scans a month; Pro includes up to 200 a month. Limits reset on the 1st of each month (UTC).
  • Refunds are handled by Apple under its policies.
