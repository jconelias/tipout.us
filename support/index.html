How does Scan receipt work?

Tap + and choose Scan receipt, then hold your phone over your checkout. The camera takes the photo by itself once it’s steady (the shutter button works too). TipOut’s AI reads the printed checkout, or the Shift Review on a Toast tablet or phone, and shows every number on one page. Tap any number to pick the right line or type it; TipOut remembers your checkout after that. Then add any cash tips and tap Log Shift. Photos are never stored by TipOut.

Scan receipt says I’ve used all my scans

Free accounts get 10 receipt scans a month and TipOut Pro gets 200. Scans reset each month, and one-time scan packs add more right away (Settings → Get TipOut Pro). You can always enter a shift by hand with Manual entry.

I don’t know my tip-out

Open Settings and tap I don’t know my tip-out, then scan the top and bottom of a checkout. TipOut finds the tip-out line, divides it by net sales and saves that % as your usual tip-out. Server and Trainer share one tip-out; Bartender has its own. You can also do this while setting up the app.

Where do I enter my Open/Close hours?

Turn on Open/Close hours in Settings → Logging. Then This Paycheck shows an Enter time card button for each week: type the hours on your time card and TipOut adds the extra hours to your paycheck at your Open/Close rate.

How does Bar Split divide tips?

It pools card and cash tips, subtracts the tip-out, and splits the rest by the hours each bartender worked. Tip-out comes out of card and cash in proportion, and everyone’s card and cash amounts add up exactly to the pool.

My pay period totals look wrong

Check Settings → Pay schedule. TipOut groups shifts by how often you’re paid (weekly, every 2 weeks or twice a month) and the date a period started.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Settings and tap Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and every shift you’ve logged.
