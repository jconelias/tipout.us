# TipOut: a month of character reels (Nov 19 to Dec 19, 2026)

31 vertical reels (1080x1920, 11 to 17 seconds, music and sound effects already mixed in). The same file works for Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts.

**Style:** original cartoon characters drawn and animated in code (no AI image or video generators, no paid tools). The cast is Maya (server), Dex (bartender), Noah (the new guy), Rio (barback), Greg (manager), Table 12 (the guest) and Penny, the TipOut pig. See `cast-lineup.png`.

**Formats:** each reel borrows a popular short-video format (POV, “of course”, red flag/green flag, “we listen and we don’t judge”, ratings, text threads and so on). No copyrighted characters, songs or creators’ videos are copied.

**App facts shown:** snap-a-checkout scan, real hourly (tip-out out, wages in), paycheck ring, best shifts per hour, bar split by hours, year chart, CSV export, re-scan updates, cash piggy, tip-out finder, Shift Review screen scan, two-tap logging. All numbers are made-up examples. Captions say “free to download” and avoid prices and scan limits.

**Music:** CC0 tracks from OpenGameArt already used by the daily posts (posting/music/). Sound effects are synthesised in code.

**Date-sensitive:** R28 is for Thanksgiving Eve (Wed Nov 25). R17 is set on a Tuesday. R29 is a December piece. If the schedule slides, keep those three on fitting days and shuffle the rest freely.

**iPhone links:** `https://github.com/jconelias/tipout.us/raw/claude/project-thread-a88tto/downloads/month/<file>.mp4`
