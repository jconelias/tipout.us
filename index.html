Know what your shifts are worth

TipOut is an iPhone app for servers and bartenders. Log each shift’s hours and tips in seconds, or scan your checkout, and see what you take home per shift, per paycheck and per hour.

  • Card and cash tips, tip-out and wages in one place
  • This paycheck at a glance, on your pay schedule
  • Real hourly: take-home divided by hours worked

TipOut is in beta on TestFlight. The App Store release is coming soon.
