02 / Everything in one app

Your whole money picture, one shift at a time.

01 — HOME

Your paycheck, live.

The ring fills as you log. Card tips, cash, wages and tip-out add up on your own pay schedule, so you always know where this paycheck stands.

  • Card and cash tracked separately
  • Weekly, every 2 weeks or twice a month
  • Day 10 of 14, at a glance

02 — SCAN

Snap your checkout.

Take a photo of your checkout and TipOut reads the card tips, sales and tip-out. The first time, tell it which line is which. You always check the numbers before saving.

  • Long checkout? Scan the top half, then the bottom
  • Photos are never stored

03 — BAR SPLIT

Pool tips by hours.

Enter the night once, add everyone’s hours, and TipOut splits card and cash to the cent. Tip-out comes out in proportion. No calculator, no arguments.

  • Split by hours worked
  • Card and cash add up exactly to the pool

04 — INSIGHTS

Your whole year.

Take-home so far, every paycheck charted side by side, and last year right there to compare. Spot your record hourly.

  • Every paycheck, charted
  • Flip back to last year

05 — BEST NIGHTS

Which night pays best?

Rank your nights per hour or per shift, compare server and bartender shifts, and tap any day on the calendar to see what you made.

  • A calendar of every shift
  • Server vs. bartender

06 — TAX

Ready for tax season.

Your tips, tip-out and take-home, month by month for the tax year, and a one-tap CSV export when it’s time to file.

  • Export your year as CSV
