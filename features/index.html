01 — HOME

Your paycheck, live.

The ring fills as you log. Card tips, cash, wages and tip-out add up on your own pay schedule, and your real hourly shows how this paycheck stacks up, with a New Record when it beats them all.

  • Card and cash tracked separately
  • Weekly, every 2 weeks or twice a month
  • New Record when you beat every paycheck

02 — LOG

Log a shift in seconds.

Tap + and pick Scan receipt or Manual entry. Manual is a keypad built for it: hours or clock-in and clock-out, card and cash tips, sales and tip-out. Working a double? Add a second shift to the same day.

  • Hours or clock-in times
  • Doubles on the same day

03 — SCAN NEW

Snap your checkout.

Hold your phone over the checkout and TipOut takes the photo by itself. Every number comes back on one page: clock in and out, card tips, net sales and tip-out. Tap any one to fix it, and TipOut remembers that line next time.

  • Reads printed checkouts and the Toast screen
  • Same checkout every shift? It fills itself in
  • Scan a shift again and it updates, not duplicates
  • Photos are never stored

04 — CASH NEW

Feed the piggy.

Cash tips aren’t on the checkout, so TipOut asks. The piggy fills as you type, toward what you usually make in cash, and your take-home updates as you go. Beat your usual and it sparkles.

  • Take-home updates as you type
  • Sparkles when you beat your usual

05 — TIP-OUT NEW

Not sure what you tip out?

Scan one checkout and TipOut works out your tip-out as a percent of net sales, then saves it for every shift after. Server and Trainer share it; Bartender keeps its own.

  • Saved as your usual tip-out
  • Right in setup, or any time in Settings

06 — PAYCHECK

Every shift, day by day.

Scroll back through the paycheck one day at a time: hours, card and cash tips, tip-out, wages and take-home, with the real hourly for every day.

  • Average hourly for every day
  • Flip back to earlier paychecks

07 — BAR SPLIT

Pool tips by hours.

Enter the night once, add everyone’s hours, and TipOut splits card and cash to the cent. Tip-out comes out in proportion. No calculator, no arguments.

  • Split by hours worked
  • Card and cash add up exactly to the pool

08 — REAL HOURLY PRO

The number that matters.

Tips look big until you divide by the hours. TipOut Pro charts your real hourly across every pay period, with tip-out out, wages in and your record marked.

  • Trend line across your pay periods

09 — INSIGHTS PRO

Your whole year.

Take-home so far, every paycheck charted side by side, and last year right there to compare. Spot your record hourly.

  • Every paycheck, charted
  • Flip back to last year

10 — BEST NIGHTS PRO

Which night pays best?

Rank your nights per hour or per shift, compare server and bartender shifts, and tap any day on the calendar to see what you made.

  • A calendar of every shift
  • Server vs. bartender

11 — TAX PRO

Ready for tax season.

Your tips, tip-out and take-home, month by month for the tax year, and a one-tap CSV export when it’s time to file.

  • Export your year as CSV
